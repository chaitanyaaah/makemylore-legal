Host policy

Effective 2026-10-11. Policy version 2026-09-16.

Before publishing

Identify yourself accurately and describe what you actually provide: date/time, meeting point, capacity, duration, inclusions/exclusions, price, deposits, accessibility, fitness level, equipment, age restrictions and cancellation conditions. Do not imply that Find Lore has inspected a venue or insured an activity unless that is demonstrably true. Obtain the permissions, professional qualifications, insurance and licences required for your specific activity.

Examples requiring particular attention include transport, adventure activities, food service, alcohol service and public film/music screenings. A venue booking alone does not establish every required licence. Do not publish an unlawful or dangerously misleading plan. Paid hosting requires the eligibility and settlement checks shown in the app; a saved UPI ID is not confirmation that provider onboarding or KYC has completed.

During the plan

Respect capacity and admission promises. Communicate significant changes promptly. Do not misrepresent attendance or pressure a participant into being photographed, tagged, drinking alcohol, disclosing an ID publicly or doing an unsafe activity. Keep participant details for the plan's legitimate coordination; do not sell them, scrape them into a marketing list or contact members who have declined further contact.

Have a practical emergency plan proportionate to the activity. Give accurate incident information and preserve necessary evidence securely. A host cannot guarantee another person's conduct, but must respond reasonably to safety issues within the host's control.

Money and cancellations

The current host platform commission is 12% of the applicable paid-seat price. Explain taxes and included/excluded costs before booking. You are responsible for your own business/tax obligations; the platform remains responsible for its own. A tax adviser must determine any registration, invoicing, withholding or collection obligations specific to your activity and the platform model.

Do not redirect a platform booking off-app to conceal charges or evade promised refund protection. Genuine separately disclosed purchases such as a restaurant bill are distinct from an in-app seat charge. Host cancellation triggers full booking refunds under the Refund policy. Do not spend expected proceeds as if settlement is guaranteed.

Payouts depend on completed activity, verified provider arrangements, reconciliation, refunds, disputes and lawful holds. We do not promise next-day payouts or an unimplemented manual UPI payout service. If a payout is withheld, we provide a reason where lawful and a route to dispute it.

Content, moderation and exit

Obtain permission before posting identifiable people or private venues. Use reviews honestly and never make a refund conditional on deleting criticism. You can challenge moderation through the Grievance policy. Removing an account or cancelling hosting privileges does not eliminate outstanding participant refunds, genuine debts or legally required records.
