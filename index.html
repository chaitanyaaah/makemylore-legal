Inside the app

Everything a plan needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

Plan chat

Every plan has a group chat from the moment you are in. Break the ice, sort the details, keep the thread after.

Real tickets

A confirmed seat is a ticket with a QR code. The host scans it at the door, so the table is the people who said yes.

Split the bill

Add what was spent, see who owes what, settle by UPI and mark it paid. No spreadsheet, no awkward follow-up.

The city on a map

Every upcoming plan as a pin with its photo and price. See what is close, what is tonight, and what is worth the drive.

Hosting, made easy

Put up a plan in a minute, approve who joins, and charge a seat fee if you want. You collect it, and all of it is yours.

Lore cards

When a plan ends, turn its photos into a card worth sharing. The story is the souvenir.

A few good questions

Before you
say “I’m in.”

Where is Find Lore live?

Chandigarh first. We are starting close to home so that the community can grow around real places and shared plans. More cities will follow.

Can I join and host?

Yes. Find something you want to join, or bring an idea of your own. The same account lets you do both. Find Lore is for people aged 18 and over.

Does every plan cost money?

No. A plan can be free or have a cost set by its host. Check the price, what is included and the payment arrangement before you join.

Do I need to know anyone?

You can arrive on your own. Check the host, plan details and who is going, then use the plan chat to break the ice. Some plans need host approval before your place is confirmed.

What is a Lore card?

A way to turn a finished plan into a shared memory. Add your photos and words, choose a style, and create a card you can keep or share.

How do I get help?

Open Help in the app for anything about your account or a plan. Reporting, blocking and deleting your account all live in the app too.
