Share your Lore and recognition policy

Effective 2026-10-11. Policy version 2026-09-16.

Make the memory first

Lore cards open after an eligible plan has ended for its confirmed participants. You may customise the selected images, caption and supported layouts, save a card or send it through a sharing destination. Creating a card does not certify attendance or the truth of every caption. A saved image or share-sheet handoff does not prove a public social post.

Choose what you share

Use photos you are entitled to publish. Obtain appropriate permission from identifiable people and avoid unnecessary precise locations. Crew tags and venue fields are optional. Turning them off does not blur faces or remove names/locations already in your photo or caption. Keep identity documents, payment information and private messages off Lore cards.

Current moment files use public media URLs. Uploads go into the shared plan album, and qualifying completed public plans/moments may appear in Lore previews or public walls. Do not treat the album as a private vault. We must not use your face or content for unrelated advertising without an appropriate permission. Rights complaints can be made through the Grievance policy.

Monthly recognition

Top Lore of the month lists up to five qualifying completed plans in the selected city whose end falls in the current Indian calendar month. Each distinct eligible participant with a recorded Lore sharing action contributes one count. Repeat actions or changing sharing channels do not add counts. Poster shares do not count. Removed participants and women-only plans are excluded from this public ranking. Equal scores are ordered consistently using the plan identifier; this tie order is not a merit judgement.

The board updates as records change and resets with the calendar month. It is recognition without a cash prize, lottery entry, paid placement or guaranteed reach. Do not buy participation or manipulate accounts, attendance or records. Corrections, deletion or enforcement can change a listing; contact support about a factual error.

Host of the Month

Host of the Month is a separate live city board for verified hosts. Hosts qualify with at least two completed public plans with recorded guest attendance and three distinct guests recorded as attended, using plans ending in the current Indian calendar month. Each guest counts once per host across plans. More distinct guests rank first, followed by more completed qualifying plans, with host identifiers breaking remaining ties. Cancelled, future and women-only plans, deleting accounts and blocked hosts are excluded. The displayed leader can change before the month ends; this is not a final historical award, cash prize or safety endorsement. Attendance records can require correction and do not independently prove every real-world interaction.

Side quests and reputation

Themes are optional creative prompts, not instructions to take risks. The current theme's card sticker uses plan dates and objective criteria, such as an actual start before 7 AM IST or a trip lasting at least 48 hours. A prompt does not establish that attendees were strangers, friends or consented to promotion.

XP and badges have no cash redemption in the current product. Credit earning and redemption are paused. Any future contest with prizes needs separately published eligibility, dates, judging, prize and tax rules before entry opens. Neither Apple, Google nor a social sharing destination sponsors a Find Lore challenge unless expressly stated with authorisation.
