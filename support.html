Support and operator information

Effective 2026-10-11. Policy version 2026-09-16.

Who operates Find Lore

Legal operator: Find Lore. Address: Chandigarh, India. Support: teamfindlore@gmail.com. Grievance officer: Grievance Officer, Find Lore at teamfindlore@gmail.com. Child-safety contact: teamfindlore@gmail.com.

Before you write

Most answers are in the app: You → Settings → Help centre (or the “How it works” link on the welcome screen), joining, tickets, hosting, check-in, splitting the bill, Lore, verification, deleting your account. Search it first; if it does not answer you, the “Still stuck?” card there opens a message to us.

Signing in

Sign in with your Indian mobile number and the code we text you (or by email, Google or Apple where the app shows them). Wait for the resend timer before asking for another code; too many requests from one number or network are paused for a while. Never give a code to anyone, including someone claiming to be support. If a code never arrives, tell us the time, your masked number (+91 98xxx xx210) and your phone network.

Plans, tickets and splits

For a plan question include the plan name and date. For a ticket problem, whether you were checked in and by whom. For a split, the expense and the payment record; remember Find Lore keeps the tally but the money moves between you over UPI or cash. When paid seats are on, include the order or refund reference and do not pay again while a confirmation is pending, we reconcile the record instead of asking you to guess.

Safety and privacy

Use Report and Block in the app for unwanted contact or content, reports reach a person. For an urgent privacy or intimate-image complaint write to the grievance officer with the link or identifier of the content; do not forward the content itself. For immediate danger call 112. Data access, correction, export and deletion are described in the Privacy notice and Delete-account page.

Response times

We acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 15 days (usually much sooner); safety and privacy complaints jump the queue and follow the Grievance policy’s statutory deadlines. We do not run a 24-hour emergency line.
