Privacy notice

Effective 2026-10-11. Policy version 2026-09-21.

Who we are

Find Lore is operated by Find Lore, Chandigarh, India (the "operator", "we"). Write to teamfindlore@gmail.com for anything about your data. Our grievance officer under India's intermediary rules is Grievance Officer, Find Lore, reachable at teamfindlore@gmail.com. This notice covers the Find Lore app, its public web pages and our support channels. We are a data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and an intermediary under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its 2021 rules.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to run: your number (or email, or a Google/Apple sign-in), the profile you write, the plans you host and join, your messages, photos and reviews.
  • Other members see your profile, your plans and who is going. They never see your phone number, email, date of birth, ID documents or UPI id, except that a co-member settling a split with you, or someone holding a seat on a plan where you charge a seat fee, can see the UPI id you chose to add.
  • We do not sell your data, run ads, or track you across other apps. There is no advertising SDK in the app.
  • Your data lives on servers in Mumbai. A few services outside India receive only what they need (push delivery, map lookups, weather), listed below.
  • You can export everything, correct it, or delete your account from Settings. Deletion runs immediately.

What we collect, and why

Sign-in and account. Your phone number, and the one-time codes exchanged to verify it (codes are single-use and not kept). If you sign in with email, Google or Apple: that email and the provider's account identifier. Session tokens and the device's push-notification token. Why: to sign you in, keep one account per person, and deliver notifications. We never read your SMS inbox or contacts.

Profile. Name, handle, date of birth (we store it; others see only your age), gender, city, interests, travel style, budget preference, bio, photo, and optionally your Instagram handle, a UPI id and where you are heading next. Why: to show you to other members, decide eligibility (18+, women-only plans), and rank plans for you.

Plans and participation. Plans you host (title, description, venue, coordinates, time, seats, rules, itinerary, cover photo), seats you request and hold, whether you were checked in or marked no-show, tickets (a random secret per seat, so a ticket cannot be forged), reviews you give and receive, questions and answers, checklists, hype and saved plans, invites, and share events (which plan or Lore you shared and where). Why: to run plans, keep the reliability score honest, and settle disputes.

Where you are, on the map. When you tap the locate button on the map we ask your phone for your position once, use it on that screen to sort plans by how far away they are and to centre the map, and forget it when you leave. It is never sent to us, never stored, and you are never shown to other people on the map. Say no and every other part of the map still works.

Group chat. Messages, polls and votes, reactions, and the location you post when you tap the pin (a single point at that moment, we do not track you continuously). Messages are stored in plain form so we can show them on any of your devices and act on reports; they are not end-to-end encrypted.

Find Lore Plus. If you subscribe, we keep which store you bought through, which plan you bought, the store's own identifier for the subscription, when it runs out and whether you have cancelled. Why: to know whether to unlock the Plus features. We never see your card. The App Store or Google Play takes the payment and holds the billing relationship, so that is also where you cancel. Deleting your account deletes this record; it does not cancel the subscription, which you must do in the store.

Splitting the bill and host-collected seat fees. Expenses you add (what, how much, who is in), a seat fee a host sets, and payment records you or others mark (who paid whom, amount, UPI or cash, whether it was confirmed). We never move money in these cases. If you add a UPI id, the only people who can read it are co-members on a plan where an expense exists between you, and people holding a seat on a plan where you charge a seat fee; it is not shown on your profile or in search.

Photos. Profile photos, plan covers and Moments (photos added to a plan by people on it). These three are served from public links: only people on the plan can browse Moments inside the app, but anyone who has a file's link can open it. Treat them as you would anything posted publicly. Chat images are private to the conversation.

Who looked at a plan. When you open a plan somebody else is hosting, we record that you looked, so the host can see that people are interested. We keep one record per person per plan, never a count of visits, so neither we nor the host can tell how many times you came back or when. A host sees a number for free and, with Find Lore Plus, the faces; they never see a history. People already holding a seat are not recorded, because they joined rather than looked. You can switch this off entirely in Settings → Stay invisible when browsing, and with it off nothing is written at all, so there is nothing stored about you to show, export or delete. Records are deleted 30 days after the last visit.

Identity verification (optional). A selfie and a government ID photo, if you ask for the verified badge. They go to a private store that only reviewers can open; a person compares them and records the decision. The images are deleted 30 days after the decision by an automatic job; the decision and date are kept. A badge means the face matched the ID, it is not a background check.

Payments (switched off at launch). When checkout through Find Lore goes live, Razorpay handles the payment page; we receive the order, payment and refund references, amount, method and status, never your card number, UPI PIN or bank password. Find Lore is the seller of record for those seats and pays hosts from its own account through RazorpayX; hosts who set up payouts pass their bank or UPI details through us to the provider, and we keep the account holder name, the last four digits, IFSC or UPI id, PAN (for tax deduction at source) and the provider's account reference.

Notifications and support. What we notified you about and whether you read it, your notification preference, and anything you send support. Why: to show the bell, to stop repeating ourselves, and to answer you.

Diagnostics. Our servers log requests (timestamps, endpoint, an IP address, errors) for security and debugging. The app has no crash-analytics or advertising SDK. OTP requests are counted per number and per network address to stop abuse; those counters clear daily.

Assistants (switched off at launch). The concierge and the "write it for me" helpers run on your phone with rule-based logic today. If we turn on the hosted version, the text you type and the plan or profile details needed to answer are sent to Anthropic (USA) to generate a reply, and plan or profile text may be sent to Voyage AI (USA) to compute matching vectors. We will update this notice and tell you in the app before that happens.

What other members see

Your profile (name, photo, age, city, interests, bio, badges, reliability score, plans hosted and attended), the plans you host, and that you are going to a plan you joined. Members of a plan see its chat, expenses and Moments. People who came to a plan see each other's Instagram handles on the Lore card if they added them. Completed public plans can appear on the city's Lore wall. Nobody sees your phone number, email, date of birth, ID documents, UPI id (except as above), or who you have blocked.

Your choices and rights

  • See and export. Settings → Export my data gives you everything we hold about you as a file.
  • Correct. Edit profile changes most things yourself. Once verified, your legal name is locked to match your ID, write to support to correct it.
  • Delete. Settings → Delete account, or email teamfindlore@gmail.com from the address or number on the account. What happens is described in the Delete-account page. It cannot be undone.
  • Withdraw consent. Turn off notifications, location or camera in Settings or your phone's settings; turn off the assistants' hosted features if we switch them on; stop using a feature. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that already happened and may make a feature unavailable.
  • Nominate someone. Under the DPDP Act you may nominate a person to exercise these rights if you are unable to; write to support.
  • Complain. Write to the grievance officer at teamfindlore@gmail.com. We acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 15 days, faster where the Grievance policy sets a shorter statutory deadline. If you are not satisfied you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once its complaint route is open, or the Grievance Appellate Committee for eligible intermediary complaints, and the courts.

We verify that a request comes from the account holder in a way proportionate to the risk. Support will never ask you for a sign-in code, a UPI PIN or a password.

Children

Find Lore is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up and remove accounts we find to be under 18. Report a suspected underage account or any exploitation through the Child safety page.

Security and breaches

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with our providers; access is limited by role, identity documents sit in a private store, and every reviewer decision is logged with who made it. No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board without undue delay, as the DPDP Rules require, with what happened and what to do.

Cookies and local storage

The app and the web preview keep your sign-in, your preferences (city, light or dark, haptics, sounds), the plans you have already opened, your saved plans, an unfinished plan you were writing, and the last few things you typed into search. All of it stays on your device: the searches and the opened-plan list are never sent to us, and everything here is cleared when you sign out or delete your account. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party trackers.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new effective date, and tell you in the app before any change that uses your data for a new purpose. Continuing to use the app after a change you were told about means you accept it; if you do not, delete your account and nothing further is processed.
