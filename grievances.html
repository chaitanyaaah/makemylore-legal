Grievances, reports and appeals

Effective 2026-10-11. Policy version 2026-09-16.

Contact

Operator: Find Lore. Business address: Chandigarh, India. Grievance officer: Grievance Officer, Find Lore. Grievance email: teamfindlore@gmail.com. Consumer support: teamfindlore@gmail.com.

Use an available in-app report control or email us the relevant account/plan/content identifier, when it occurred, why you are reporting it and a safe way to respond. A person affected by content can report without holding an account. Share only the evidence needed. Do not email passwords, verification codes, PINs or copies of illegal sexual imagery.

Content complaints and urgent harm

For complaints covered by India's intermediary rules we acknowledge within 24 hours and resolve within 15 days, and we aim for seven. Content that a court or a government agency orders removed comes down within 36 hours of the order. A complaint about intimate imagery of you shared without consent, or an account impersonating you, is acted on within 24 hours of the complaint, as the rules require. Apply the statutory conditions to each category; these are not guarantees that every reported item must be removed.

Urgent valid court/government removal directions are handled through the applicable legal process, including the three-hour deadline where it applies. Evidence subject to lawful preservation is restricted rather than casually destroyed. Emergencies should be reported directly to emergency services; in India call 112.

Consumer and privacy requests

Booking/payment complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and addressed within one month under the applicable e-commerce framework; use a shorter applicable deadline when a complaint also falls within a faster category. Privacy requests receive verification proportionate to their risk and an explanation of any lawful refusal or retention. We do not ask you to disclose a login OTP to support.

Outcomes and appeals

Where lawful, we explain the action taken, key reason and how to request review. Reply to the decision with the case reference and relevant new information. Statutory escalation remains available, including the Grievance Appellate Committee route for eligible intermediary complaints, generally within 30 days of the communicated decision, and appropriate consumer authorities or courts. National Consumer Helpline information is available at https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/.

There is no requirement to give up a refund claim or statutory remedy to obtain support. A good-faith complaint is not itself grounds for retaliation. Deliberate misuse of reporting can be addressed proportionately.
