Community guidelines

Effective 2026-10-11. Policy version 2026-09-16.

Make plans people can trust

Use accurate eligibility information, describe plans honestly and cancel early when you cannot attend. Do not fake reviews, verification, availability, discounts, attendance or social popularity. A verification badge is not a reason to ignore your own safety judgement.

Respect people online and in person

No threats, stalking, bullying, coercion, hateful abuse, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual content, doxxing, impersonation or repeated contact after someone asks you to stop. Respect lawful eligibility and women-only plan rules without using them to harass or investigate another member. Do not post private IDs, bank details, home addresses or someone else's messages without appropriate permission.

Keep content and activities lawful

Do not facilitate exploitation, illegal goods/services, fraud, non-consensual intimate imagery, dangerous criminal activity or unlawful discrimination. Do not infringe copyright, trademarks or privacy. Do not fabricate evidence or use a synthetic likeness/voice to deceive people. Preserve relevant disclosure labels on materially synthetic content. Ordinary card layouts and cropping do not excuse false claims about the underlying event.

Find Lore does not permit pornography, sexual solicitation or child sexual abuse/exploitation in any form. The separate Child Safety policy applies even though membership is adult-only. Do not upload or forward illegal imagery to prove a complaint; send identifiers or links through the reporting channel.

Photos and Lore

Ask before publishing identifiable people, tagging an account or exposing a sensitive venue. Choose photos you have rights to use. A public URL or social share can travel beyond the original group. Do not assume that deleting your account removes copies others have saved. Respect takedown requests and use support when consent is disputed.

Reports, blocks and enforcement

Use available in-app report/block controls or contact teamfindlore@gmail.com with the relevant profile, plan, message or content link. Do not make abusive or deliberately false reports. Blocking restricts supported interactions; it is not a guarantee that the person cannot see a public file or content posted elsewhere.

We can remove content, restrict features, suspend accounts or refer matters to competent authorities when appropriate. We consider seriousness, evidence, repeated behaviour and safety. Urgent intervention may precede notice; where lawful and practical, we explain the decision and offer review. Moderation does not automatically cancel a member's valid refund rights.

For immediate danger in India call 112. Find Lore's report queue is not an emergency dispatch service.
