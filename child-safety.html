Child safety standards

Effective 2026-10-11. Policy version 2026-09-16.

Zero tolerance for exploitation

Find Lore prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, sexual solicitation involving children, trafficking, sexual extortion and child sexual abuse material, including synthetic depictions. Do not create, upload, request, distribute or link to such material. Do not use the service to arrange access to children or evade a safety restriction.

Report safely

Report through the app where available or contact teamfindlore@gmail.com. Provide the content/account link or identifier and a brief description. Do not download, forward or attach suspected illegal imagery to a report. Someone without a Find Lore account may report by email. In immediate danger in India call 112 or contact local police.

Response

The safety contact is teamfindlore@gmail.com. We promptly assess reports, restrict access to offending content/accounts, preserve evidence only through a lawful restricted process and report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authority as required by law. The operator must maintain an operational authority-reporting procedure and designate a child-safety contact in its app-store declarations before launch.

Underage accounts and review

People under 18 may not use Find Lore. Report suspected underage accounts without publicly posting the person's ID or exposing the child. We may restrict an account while reviewing age information and remove an ineligible account with lawful preservation of necessary records. An affected adult may challenge an incorrect decision through the Grievance policy. An adult-only age gate does not replace moderation or these standards.
